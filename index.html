How it works

Four steps from signing up to teaching

01

Create Your Profile

Verify with your university email, add your department and year, and say what you're working on this term.

02

Add Your Skills

List what you can teach and what you want to learn. Both sides of your profile power your matches.

03

Find a Mentor

Browse peers by skill, department or rating, then request a session at a time that fits your timetable.

04

Learn & Share

Every session and answer earns points and reviews that build a reputation your peers can trust.

Platform

Everything a peer learning community needs

Browse all skills →

Peer Mentorship

Request one-to-one help from students who have already passed the course you're taking.

Skill Matching

Your learning list is matched against what peers teach, so the right names surface first.

Learning Sessions

Book, reschedule and confirm sessions on campus or online, with reminders for both students.

Mentor & Learning Points

Teaching earns mentor points, learning earns learning points — both count on your profile.

Community Knowledge Sharing

Post tutorials, project write-ups and resources so answers stay available to the next cohort.

Reputation & Leaderboard

Ratings and points roll up into department and campus-wide leaderboards each term.

Community

The questions you were about to ask, already answered

Students share tutorials, project experiences, learning resources and open discussions. Useful posts rise through peer votes and stay searchable by skill tag.
